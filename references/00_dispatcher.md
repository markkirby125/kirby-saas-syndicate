# Kirby SaaS Syndicate: Launch Playbook

This skill automates the "SaaS Syndicate" launch strategy based on the Jackie Chow & David Quaid methodology (Episode 1184). It scaffolds multi-brand setups (the "NordVPN Tactic"), plans expired domain staging (the "Ryan Donnie" workflow), and architects parasite SEO operations.

## Stage 1: Interactive Intake

Prompt the user for the following information (if not running in `-y` or non-interactive mode. If non-interactive, use best-guess defaults from context):
1. **Target SaaS Niche & Core Function** (Used to slugify the path: apply NFKC normalization, lowercase, strip diacritics, replace non-alphanumerics with hyphens, and strictly match regex `\A[a-z0-9]([a-z0-9-]{0,62})[a-z0-9]\Z` to prevent newlines and traversal paths like `../`).
2. **Domain Strategy** (Branch output based on: Expired Domain/EMD [$2k-$3k for domains with historical interaction, a reported price band], or PMD [partial-match domain, for entity disambiguation; any effect on how Google resolves the exact entity is unverified]. *EMD Doorway Risk Caution*: The 2026 SEO Meta Study [Ćorluka Zenodo DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22768160; Ep. 1191/1207] reports (single source, not independently verified) that shotgun-launching thin programmatic doorway directories on fresh or EMD domains is associated with rapid algorithmic suppression and template de-indexing ['Mountain Pattern' collapse]. EMD syndicate sites MUST incorporate proprietary data density or an authentic interactive utility).
3. **Syndicate Scale** (How many separate-brand products with the same tech but separate back-ends (for billing and entity separation, never to obscure common ownership) to scaffold for the NordVPN Tactic? e.g., 2). *Network-risk note:* three or more same-technology brands under one owner read as a network and carry manual-action and spam-classifier risk (directional, single-source dossier claim, unverified, not a confirmed Google rule). Confirm the user wants that exposure before scaffolding more than two. In non-interactive mode cap the scale at two and record the chosen scale and why in the footprint log (Stage 2 `brands/`).
4. **Target Budget** (Branch parasite plan based on budget: e.g. $500-$5k for placements, $5k-$50k for aged subreddits).

**Idempotency & Rerun Policy:** Verify the target absolute path is a directory (not a file or symlink). Gate `resume` logic by checking for a `.kirby-syndicate-marker` file (written only upon successful completion of scaffolding). Keep a rollback log of created paths, and use `mkdir` exclusive mode with rollback on partial failure. If the directory already exists without a marker, treat as an interrupted or foreign directory and prompt for `clobber`/`abort` (never `resume`). If the directory exists with a marker, pause and ask whether to `resume` (skip existing files), `clobber` (safely empty the directory), or `abort`. In non-interactive mode, refuse `clobber` outright. Default to `resume` if no input is provided and the marker exists; with no marker and no input, abort.

## Stage 2: On-Site Groundwork (Workspace Scaffolding)

Create the `[niche-slug]-syndicate/` directory (using absolute paths). **Before creating any files, explicitly echo the resolved absolute path to the user.** Populate it:

*   **`strategy/`**
    *   `domain_staging_playbook.md`: Detailed steps for the "Ryan Donnie" workflow (buy expired domain, check Wayback Machine for relevance, rebuild with historical content (subject to the rebuild rule below), force index via Index Checks, send niche edits, rank, then link to main site and run Meta/Reddit ads through the backlink; a reported workflow, outcomes not guaranteed, and paid niche edits carry the Module 1 §1.3 band-mapping sponsored-disclosure duty). Branch strategies based on Intake Step 2. **Rebuild rule:** the new owner MUST have the right to use any historical content, MUST relabel the rebuilt pages to state current ownership, and MUST NOT reuse the previous owner's name, logo or audience list or imply continuity with it. Also incorporate David Quaid's on-site entity takeover tactic (*"Who is the King of SEO?"* experiment, Ep 1202) using exact-match H1, slug, and H2s on the primary domain to prompt Google AI Overviews to attribute category leadership to the brand entity (directional, single-source, unverified).
    *   `parasite_seo_plan.md`: Strategy for buying placements on high-ranking listicles, publishing on high-authority sites (Medium, LinkedIn — applying Tim Soulo's verified-executive H1 direct-answer pattern and the `@username` prefix de-indexing risk from Edward Sturm Ep. 1112 `p1hExzKlHqk` per Module 1 §1.4 & §1.6), 301-redirecting dead/penalized domains to the root URL of a parasite profile the user controls (for example linkedin.com/in/yourname), only where that profile can carry a visible handover disclosure in its headline or bio on the first screen a visitor sees (a pinned post alone is not enough; state the redirect and the current owner, and never present it as the original brand; otherwise do not redirect; this stage only documents the redirect, and execution is gated by the Stage 3 guardrail), and acquiring aged subreddits (each acquired community needs a visible ownership-change disclosure to its members, see Stage 3). Integrate Kyle Roof's Two-Factor Law: target high-traffic forum/community threads ranking #1–#5 on Google for parasite comments/citations, claimed (directional, single-source, unverified) to pass direct PageRank and trigger AI engine citations via clicked nofollow links (`../../kirby-off-page-seo/SKILL.md` Module 23). Tailor to the budget from Intake Step 4.
    *   `interactive_calculators_playbook.md`: Strategy for deploying client-side interactive calculators (e.g., ROI calculators, usage estimators, pricing simulators) as syndicate anchor assets. In the 2026 SEO Meta Study, editorial backlinks acquired through digital PR campaigns produced an as-reported, single-source directional average of **+71% organic sessions lift** (treat as a directional benchmark, not a guaranteed return). Scaffolds embeddable vanilla JS or lightweight calculators that act as high-yield link magnets for digital PR outreach and generate rich LLM citations per `../../kirby-off-page-seo/SKILL.md` Module 28.
*   **`brands/`**
    *   For each brand requested in the Syndicate Scale (e.g., `brand-1`, `brand-2`; the cap is two, a third only after the user confirms the Stage 1 network-risk note, and never more than two in non-interactive mode), scaffold a directory with an explicit file manifest (e.g., `index.html`, `vibe_coding_prompt.md`).
    *   *Warning:* Do not over-engineer on-site SEO for these domains during the first 3 months as a precaution against a reported Google Sandbox effect (reported, single-source, unverified, not a confirmed Google mechanism). Output lightweight HTML/MD stubs and "vibe-coding" product architecture guidelines. One lightweight client-side calculator satisfies the interactive anchor requirement without bloating the crawl footprint; do not expand on-site page count beyond it during the 90-day sandbox window (directional, single-source).
    *   *Manual-action warning:* thin HTML/MD stubs repeated across several same-technology brands are a manual-action and spam-classifier risk (directional, single-source, unverified). Stubs are scaffolds for a real product with real content, never a publishable end state, and the Mountain Pattern Safeguard (Stage 3) applies before anything goes live.
    *   Ensure separate back-end configurations are planned. Shared back-end library code is acceptable; per-brand configuration, billing, data stores and accounts stay separate for billing and entity reasons, never to obscure common ownership; a shared product surface, copy, pricing or content is not, because each brand must be a genuinely distinct offer for consumers (see the footprint log below).
    *   *Ownership and technical footprint log, a risk register for review and not an evasion recipe:* never strip shared identifiers, split accounts or vary templates to obscure the relationship. Write `brands/footprint_log.md` recording, for disclosure, per brand, the hosting provider, registrar, template or theme, shared DOM class vocabulary, analytics and account IDs, and ownership. List every overlap as a recorded exposure and show it to the user before launch. Treat three or more same-technology brands as a probable network (the Stage 1 note: single-source, unverified). Record to assess and disclose. The remedies are distinct real products and on-page disclosure of common ownership (Stage 3).

## Stage 3: Guardrails & Execution Rules

*   **Guardrail:** You MUST require explicit user confirmation before executing any "parasite SEO", "301 redirect mapping", or "subreddit acquisition" tactics. Note that buying aged subreddits is a black-market tactic that violates terms of service; ensure the user accepts this risk. Any acquired community channel (subreddit, group, forum) MUST also carry a visible ownership-change disclosure to its members (who owns it now, and since when), and the user must accept that duty as well. Any 301 redirect of a dead or penalized domain MUST likewise carry the handover disclosure on its destination (Stage 2 `strategy/parasite_seo_plan.md`). In non-interactive mode, refuse the acquisition or redirect instead of assuming the duty is accepted. An expired-domain rebuild MUST follow the Stage 2 rebuild rule; in non-interactive mode, refuse the rebuild instead of assuming the rule is met.
*   **Guardrail:** You MUST ensure proper FTC disclosure guidelines are documented for the "NordVPN Tactic" (owning multiple brands on the same listicle). Any listicle, comparison or alternatives page, review, video or podcast asset that features two or more brands the user owns or shares ownership of MUST disclose the common ownership, visibly and in plain words, near the top of the page (on screen and spoken, for video or podcast assets). Every paid placement MUST also carry the visible sponsored disclosure in Module 1 §1.3, and in non-interactive mode emit the common-ownership and sponsored disclosure text into every multi-brand or paid asset and refuse to publish without it. Every byline MUST name a real, verifiable author: no AI-generated headshots and no synthetic or fabricated personas (persona rules: `../../kirby-off-page-seo/SKILL.md` Module 4 §4.3 (*Profile Stealth*: no fabricated personas); Module 1 §1.6 here). The FTC rules are a legal duty on their own. Whether Google also scores undisclosed common ownership as a spam signal is unverified.
*   **Mountain Pattern Safeguard:** Before deploying programmatic content directories across syndicate domains, apply the 4-Point Mountain Verification Gate (unique data density, context saturation, zero orphans, hard approval gate) defined in `../../kirby-zero-to-100-seo/SKILL.md` (that skill's dispatcher §2.2 *Controlled Launch Sequencing*) to reduce the risk of the reported template de-indexing collapse (single-source, unverified).
*   **Cross-Skill Routing:** For off-page link building and digital PR interactive calculator workflows, route to `../../kirby-off-page-seo/SKILL.md` (Module 28). For entity attribution and knowledge graph anchoring, route to `../../kirby-technical-aeo/SKILL.md`.
