### **1.1 State of Parasite SEO (Post-Sept 2026)**
Leveraging established third-party domain authority and UGC ranking signals bypasses the fresh-site sandbox, letting pages rank same-day for competitive commercial queries and capturing AI Overview citations. Google's Site Reputation Abuse (SRA) enforcement struck **unmoderated third-party commercial leases on legacy mainstream media** (white-label directory rentals). Contextual placements, moderated UGC platforms, and developer hubs remain open and thriving. AI Overviews favor high-authority parasite platforms for commercial queries — even when the parasite URL sees organic rank volatility, LLM citation algorithms continue treating it as a consensus source.

### **1.2 Google Domain Parasites Playbook**
Ranking directly on Google-owned ecosystems, where internal domain trust overrides aggressive spam filtering:
*   **Surfaces:** Google Cloud/Firebase storage buckets and sites, Google Scripts web apps, Google Sites, YouTube video transcripts, and Google developer docs properties.
*   **Doctrine:** Publish genuinely useful content (calculators, comparison pages, documentation-style resources) on these properties for commercial queries; the host's internal trust carries retrieval. Treat as one layer of a multi-platform redundancy strategy — never a single point of failure.

### **1.3 Platform Selection Matrix — Free vs. Paid**

| Tier | Platforms | Use When |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | LinkedIn Pulse, Reddit, Medium, GitHub, Spotify podcast transcripts, Dev.to, Substack | Budget ≤ ~$1,000; you have production capacity for standalone-value content and can wait for platform-native indexation |
| **Paid** | High-tier press releases, sponsored news placements, regional newswires with guaranteed indexing | Budget ~$5,000; speed and indexation certainty matter; vet outlets against the newswire criteria in `../../kirby-off-page-seo/SKILL.md` Module 21 |

**~$1,000 model:** 100% free platforms, manual publishing, UGC-native formatting, indexation driven by internal linking between your own parasite properties. **~$5,000 model:** mix of free UGC anchors plus 2–4 paid guaranteed-indexation placements acting as the crawl entry points, with tier-2 links pointed at the free properties.

### **1.4 The 5-Step Production Pipeline**
1.  **Offer Selection:** High-EPC affiliate programs (SaaS, financial, crypto, lead-gen) with substantial payouts — the platform takes a conversion cut, so bounty must survive it.
2.  **Low-Competition SERP Spotting:** Target commercial queries whose top 10 contains forum threads, outdated blog posts, or weak niche sites.
3.  **Platform Selection:** Apply §1.3 matrix per query intent.
4.  **On-Page Intent Optimization:** Exact-match title structures, high-density topic-comment opening hooks, scannable commercial comparison tables.
5.  **Canonical Management & Indexation:** Detect and bypass platform-level canonical bugs (self-referential or parent-category canonicals — audit SOP in `../../kirby-off-page-seo/SKILL.md` Module 21); drive indexation via tier-2 link networks.

### **1.5 Internal Link Arbitrage SOP**
Buying or building **internal links within the parasite host domain** often outperforms expensive tier-1 external backlink campaigns:
1.  Map the host domain's own pages that already rank adjacent to your target query.
2.  Acquire links from those pages to your parasite page (platform-native means: comments where moderated, related-article placements, profile-to-article links, category listings).
3.  This transfers the host's own page-level relevance, not just domain authority — cheaper and more durable than external equivalents.

### **1.6 Parasite CRO Framework (The Conversion Dilemma)**
Parasite pages convert below native brand sites due to platform framing. Required stack:
*   **Embedded comparison widgets** (interactive, not screenshots) so the commercial value is consumed on-page.
*   **Explicit visual CTA blocks** with a single dominant action.
*   **Clear disclaimers** (affiliate relationship, FTC) — trust deficit is the conversion killer; disclosure raises it.
*   **High-trust bridge funnels:** link to a neutral comparison/brand page the user can verify, rather than demanding the conversion in-platform.
