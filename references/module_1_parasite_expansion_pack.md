# Module 1: Parasite Expansion Pack — Google Domain Parasites, Platform Matrix, Arbitrage & CRO

*Source: Charles Floate parasite-SEO masterclass (The Edward Show, Episode E1193, post-September 2026). Compliance boundary for every tactic here: `../../kirby-aiseo-skill/SKILL.md` Module 15 §15.3 — if a placement's only value is borrowed host authority with no editorial mediation or standalone merit, it is on the abusive side of SRA and this skill does not build it.*

### **1.1 State of Parasite SEO (Post-Sept 2026)**
Leveraging established third-party domain authority and UGC ranking signals bypasses the fresh-site sandbox, letting pages rank same-day for competitive commercial queries and capturing AI Overview citations. Google's Site Reputation Abuse (SRA) enforcement struck **unmoderated third-party commercial leases on legacy mainstream media** (white-label directory rentals) — and paid listicle/advertorial placements on legacy outlets fall on the struck side whenever they are unmoderated commercial leases rather than editorially reviewed placements. Contextual placements, moderated UGC platforms, and developer hubs remain open and thriving. AI Overviews favor high-authority parasite platforms for commercial queries — even when the parasite URL sees organic rank volatility, LLM citation algorithms continue treating it as a consensus source.

### **1.2 Google Domain Parasites Playbook**
Ranking directly on Google-owned ecosystems, where internal domain trust extends retrieval to utility content:
*   **Surfaces:** Google Cloud/Firebase storage buckets and sites, Google Scripts web apps, Google Sites, YouTube video transcripts, and Google developer docs properties.
*   **Doctrine:** Publish genuinely useful content (calculators, comparison pages, documentation-style resources) on these properties for commercial queries; the host's internal trust carries retrieval **only for content with standalone utility** — thin commercial pages on Google properties face the same abuse classification as anywhere else (§1.1 boundary test). Treat as one layer of a multi-platform redundancy strategy — never a single point of failure.

### **1.3 Platform Selection Matrix — Free vs. Paid**

| Tier | Platforms | Use When |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | LinkedIn Pulse, Reddit, Medium, GitHub, Spotify podcast transcripts, Dev.to, Substack | Budget ≤ ~$1,000; you have production capacity for standalone-value content and can wait for platform-native indexation |
| **Free (Google-domain)** | Google Cloud/Firebase, Google Scripts, Google Sites, YouTube transcripts (§1.2 — utility content only) | Query intent matches a tool/calculator/documentation resource; standalone utility is the editorial-mediation substitute |
| **Paid** | High-tier press releases, sponsored news placements, regional newswires with guaranteed indexing | Budget ~$5,000; speed and indexation certainty matter; vet outlets against the newswire criteria in `../../kirby-off-page-seo/SKILL.md` Module 21 |

**Band mapping (aligned to Stage 1 intake Step 4):** $500–$5k intake ➔ free-first: free platforms + Google-domain utility assets, manual publishing, UGC-native formatting, indexation driven by internal linking between your own parasite properties; scale paid placements into the upper half of the band only for load-bearing queries. $5k–$50k intake ➔ mixed: free UGC anchors plus paid guaranteed-indexation placements acting as crawl entry points, tier-2 links at the free properties, and the aged-subreddit branch (dispatcher Stage 2) where the budget qualifies. Any paid placement must carry platform-compliant sponsored disclosure (`rel=sponsored` where the platform marks links) — paid link value without qualification is a compliance defect, not a bargain.

### **1.4 The 5-Step Production Pipeline**
1.  **Offer Selection:** High-EPC affiliate programs (SaaS, financial, crypto, lead-gen) with substantial payouts — the platform takes a conversion cut, so bounty must survive it. *(When running this pipeline under this skill, extend the Stage 1 intake with an offer/payout question — the default intake does not collect it.)*
2.  **Low-Competition SERP Spotting:** Target commercial queries whose top 10 contains forum threads, outdated blog posts, or weak niche sites.
3.  **Platform Selection:** Apply §1.3 matrix per query intent.
4.  **On-Page Intent Optimization:** Exact-match title structures, high-density topic-comment opening hooks, scannable commercial comparison tables.
5.  **Canonical Management & Indexation:** Detect platform-level canonical bugs (self-referential or parent-category canonicals) and route to the canonical-audit SOP in `../../kirby-off-page-seo/SKILL.md` Module 21 — remediate via platform-native fixes or shift budget off the platform; do not attempt to 'bypass' a canonical you do not control. Drive indexation via tier-2 link networks.

### **1.5 Internal Link Arbitrage SOP**
Buying or building **internal links within the parasite host domain** often outperforms expensive tier-1 external backlink campaigns:
1.  Map the host domain's own pages that already rank adjacent to your target query.
2.  Acquire links from those pages to your parasite page via platform-native, moderated means (comments where moderated, related-article placements); self-serve profile/category links only where platform ToS allows and the placement survives editorial review — mass non-editorial self-placement is the SRA pattern of §1.1.
3.  This transfers the host's own page-level relevance, not just domain authority — cheaper and more durable than external equivalents.

### **1.6 Parasite CRO Framework (The Conversion Dilemma)**
Parasite pages convert below native brand sites due to platform framing. Required stack:
*   **Embedded comparison widgets** (interactive, not screenshots) so the commercial value is consumed on-page.
*   **Explicit visual CTA blocks** with a single dominant action.
*   **Clear disclaimers** (affiliate relationship, FTC) — trust deficit is the conversion killer; disclosure raises it.
*   **High-trust bridge funnels:** link to a neutral comparison/brand page the user can verify, rather than demanding the conversion in-platform.
